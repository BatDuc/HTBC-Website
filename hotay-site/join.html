Become a Member

Rowers of all ages and abilities are welcome. Come down, try rowing and join the club.

How to Join

Ho Tay Boat Club is a friendly, welcoming community. We ask every member, coach and guest to treat each other with respect and kindness, whatever their age, background or experience, so the club stays a great place to row.

Get in touch

Call +84 913 208 101, email us, message the club on Facebook, or use the form below.

Try rowing

Book a single experience session, or start with the beginner rowing course with a coach.

Choose a membership

Pick the option that suits you from the fees below. Regular and weekend memberships are paid for 6 months or a year.

Pay and start rowing

Pay by bank transfer to the club's account. We will send you the details. Then you are ready to row.
