Welcome

Ho Tay Boat Club (HTBC) is an amateur club for people who love boating and water activities, with a great affection for Hanoi's West Lake. We started with rowing at our core and have grown strongly into dragon boat and SUP, with sailing, windsurfing and kayaking also available.

We are proud of our international coaching and our careful safety practices: our rowing, kayak and SUP coaches are international or national medalists or certified trainers. Locals, expatriates and visitors all make HTBC their home for recreation, training, team building and competition.
